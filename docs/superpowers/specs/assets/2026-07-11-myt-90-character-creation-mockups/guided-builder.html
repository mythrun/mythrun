The one design question on this screen How deep should the sequence go? My proposal: Calling → Practice → Origin → Anything else, where Practice (subclass) only appears for classes that actually choose it at level 1 (Cleric, Sorcerer, Warlock in the SRD) and is skipped automatically for the rest — no fake choices. Origin bundles species + background into one step. Notes are optional/skippable. Lighter (drop Practice entirely) or deeper (species and background as separate steps) are both on the table.
