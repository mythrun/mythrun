Generation — two acts (chaos → order)

Act 1: entities coalesce from pixel-chaos, swirl thins to nothing by the last one. Act 2: the web stitches in, slow penstrokes.

speed:
  • Swirl — dense & pixel-y at the start (chaos), thinning on a curve, gone by the final entity. Quantity = Act-1 progress.
  • Act 2 edges — after all nodes are in, connections draw as slow deliberate penstrokes (~2s each), one at a time.
  • A couple of edges snap at the very end (the coherence repair).
