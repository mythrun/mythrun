World & Campaign

Prep engine, world store, campaign pipeline.

The first slices are built. Each world carries a world colour identity — a two-stop dither pair a Sonnet stage picks at world creation and code contrast-guards, stored on the world and worn as its campaign cards' cover art. The campaign hub payload (GET /api/campaigns/mine) assembles everything a home-hub card needs — the world, the party, the play record, and a per-viewer status — in two batched queries, feeding the campaign hub screen. The broader prep engine and world store grow in as the pipeline ships.
