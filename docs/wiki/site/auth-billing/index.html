Auth & Billing

How a player becomes a signed-in user, how that identity travels with their requests, how their session credit is metered, and how Stripe fits in.

Four pages in this section so far: Identity — the Cognito User Pool, the PKCE public App Client, the Hosted UI domain, and the Google + Discord OIDC providers that back federated sign-in; JWT middleware — the Fastify plugin family that validates Cognito access tokens at the edge, the bypass mode for local dev with its six-layer defense against accidental activation, and the requireUser narrowing helper protected handlers reach through; Onboarding & invites — the first-login flow that creates the users row, the requireOnboarded gate that pins every protected route on that row's existence, and the six invite routes that let a DM-owner pull others into a campaign by sharing a 12-character code; and Stripe contract — the closed enum of lookup_keys, the cached SDK client pinned to one API version, the read-only boot probe that fails closed if Stripe's catalog drifts, the restricted-key boundary that makes price mutation impossible on the wire, and the ESLint AST tripwires above it. The credits ledger and Stripe webhooks land as those layers ship.
