Privacy

Last updated DATE

The short version. Mythrun keeps your account, your purchases, and your campaigns. It sends what you write to a model provider so the game can answer. It does not sell your data, does not show advertising, and does not run analytics or tracking scripts.

Who is responsible

LEGAL ENTITY NAME, REGISTERED ADDRESS, is the controller of the personal data described here. Questions and requests go to privacy@mythrun.com.

What is collected

Account

When you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your email address and the basic profile that provider shares. You choose a username and a display name. Mythrun never receives or stores your Google or Discord password.

What you play

Prompts, session transcripts, characters, campaign and world state, invites, and the record of who is in which campaign. This is the product. It is stored so a campaign can continue across sessions.

Payment

Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach Mythrun's servers. Mythrun stores an identifier for your Stripe customer record, your plan status, and a history of purchases and credit grants, which is what the account page shows you.

Technical

Server logs of requests, including IP address, browser user agent, and timestamps, plus error diagnostics. These are used to keep the service running and to investigate abuse and faults.

Cookies

Mythrun sets one cookie: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in. It is httpOnly, so scripts cannot read it, and it is not sent to other sites. There are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers on this website or in the application.

Why it is used

  • To run the service you asked for, which is the performance of our contract with you.
  • To take payment and keep the records tax law requires.
  • To keep the service safe and working, which is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and diagnosing faults.
  • To answer you when you write to support.

Who it is shared with

Mythrun does not sell personal data. It is shared with the services that run the product:

  • Amazon Web Services, hosting, databases, and sign-in.
  • Anthropic, which generates the narration. Your prompts and the relevant campaign context are sent to its API to produce a response.
  • Stripe, payments and subscription billing.
  • Google and Discord, only for signing you in.

Content sent to the model provider is processed to return a response. It is not used to train models. CONFIRM against the current Anthropic commercial terms before publishing.

Data may also be disclosed where the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Where it is held

Mythrun runs in AWS REGION. If you are outside that region, your data is transferred there and protected by TRANSFER MECHANISM, e.g. standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept

  • Account and campaign data, for as long as your account exists.
  • After you close your account, campaigns and transcripts are deleted within PERIOD. Content you wrote in a shared campaign may remain in that campaign's record for the other players.
  • Billing records are kept for PERIOD, because tax and accounting law requires it.
  • Server logs are kept for PERIOD.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and ask for your data in a portable form. Write to privacy@mythrun.com and we will answer within PERIOD, e.g. 30 days.

If you are in the UK or the EU and you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the right to know what is collected, to have it deleted, and not to be treated differently for asking. Mythrun does not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California law.

Children

Mythrun is not intended for children under MINIMUM AGE. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, it will be closed and the data deleted.

Changes
