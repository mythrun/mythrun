2. What Mythrun is

Mythrun is a subscription and pay-as-you-go service that runs tabletop roleplaying sessions for small groups. It generates a fictional world from your description, narrates play, voices the characters in it, resolves combat under fifth edition rules, and keeps a record of what happened between sessions. The narration and world material are produced by large language models.

Mythrun is entertainment. Nothing it writes is advice, fact, or a statement about a real person, place, or event, even when it resembles one.

4. Credits and payment

Play is metered in credits. A world credit creates one world and the campaign that runs in it. A session credit plays one session, and is spent by the campaign host, not by the other players. Current prices are on the pricing page.

  • Credits included with a monthly plan are granted at the start of each billing period and expire at the end of that period. They do not carry over.
  • Credits bought as one-off packs do not expire.
  • Credits have no cash value, cannot be transferred between accounts, and cannot be exchanged for money except as set out in the refund policy.
  • Plans renew automatically each month at the then-current price until you cancel. Payment is taken by our payment processor at the start of each period.
  • Prices can change. A change to the price of a plan you are already on takes effect at your next renewal, and you will be told before it does.

Cancellation and refunds are covered separately in refunds and cancellation.

A campaign is shared. Anything you write in a session is visible to the other players in that campaign and stays in its record, including after you leave.
