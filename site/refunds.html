Refunds and cancellation

Last updated DATE

Mythrun sells digital credits. If something has gone wrong, write to support@mythrun.com before anything else. Most problems are fixed the same day.

Cancelling a plan

You can cancel a monthly plan at any time from your account, with no notice period and no cancellation fee. Cancelling stops the next payment.

  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • The credits granted for that period stay spendable until the period ends. They are not taken away early.
  • At the end of the period, unspent plan credits expire, as they do every month.
  • Credits you bought separately as packs are not affected by cancelling. They do not expire.
  • Cancelling part-way through a period does not produce a partial refund of that period.

Your account and your campaigns stay. You can come back later, or delete the account entirely.

Refunds

A purchase can be refunded within 14 days if the credits it granted are still unspent.

  • Refunds are for the full amount of a purchase. Mythrun does not issue partial refunds against a purchase.
  • A refund reverses the credits that purchase granted. If those credits have already been spent on a world or a session, the purchase cannot be refunded, because the thing it paid for has been delivered.
  • A monthly payment can be refunded within 14 days if none of that period's credits have been spent.

Outside that, refunds are at our discretion, and we use it. If a session failed, produced nothing usable, or was interrupted by a fault on our side, tell us and we will refund the credit or the payment. You do not need to argue the case.

If we close your account for a reason that is not your fault, unused credits you paid for are refunded.

How to ask

Email support@mythrun.com from the address on your account and say what you bought and what went wrong. We reply within RESPONSE TIME, e.g. 2 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and usually appear within five to ten business days, depending on your bank.

Your statutory rights

If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU you normally have fourteen days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content. That right ends once delivery begins with your consent, and you consent to immediate delivery when you spend a credit. Until you spend it, the right stands, and this policy does not limit it. Disputes and chargebacks

If you do not recognise a charge, or you think it is wrong, please write to us first. We can see the purchase, tell you what it was, and refund it if it should not have happened. That is faster than a bank dispute, which takes weeks.

If a chargeback is raised, the account may be suspended while it is resolved, and we will respond to your bank with the record of the purchase and the credits it granted. Once a chargeback is settled, the account is restored or closed depending on the outcome.

Chargebacks raised without contacting us first, on purchases that were used, may result in the account being closed.
