Pricing

Two things cost money. Starting a world, and playing a session. Joining someone else's campaign, making a character, and reading back your own transcripts cost nothing.

What a credit buys

World credit

Creates one world and the campaign that runs inside it. It covers generating the world and building a character for everyone at the table.

Session credit

Plays one session. The host spends it. The other players at the table spend nothing.

Monthly plans

Plan Per month Worlds Sessions
Wayfarer $15 1 4
Sojourner $25 2 8
Mythwright $45 4 16

Plan credits arrive at the start of each billing period and expire at the end of it. They do not carry over. Plans renew monthly until cancelled, and can be cancelled at any time.

One at a time

No plan required. Buy what you need, when you need it.

Pack Price Grants
Session $X 1 session
World $X 1 world and 1 session

Credits bought this way never expire.

Taxes and currency

Prices are in US dollars and exclude / include any sales tax or VAT, which is calculated at checkout where it applies.

Changing or stopping

Plans can be changed or cancelled at any time from your account. See refunds and cancellation for what happens to credits when you do.
