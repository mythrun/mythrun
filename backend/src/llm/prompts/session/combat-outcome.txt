Set an appropriate DC (Difficulty Class) for the party to win the fight, and write four outcome descriptions — one for each result tier. Each outcome describes what happens to the PARTY at that tier:

- "decisive" — the party wins cleanly: little to no lasting harm, control of the field, the enemy broken or driven off.
- "costly" — the party wins, but takes real injuries: spent resources, bloodied PCs, a hard-fought victory.
- "pyrrhic" — the party still wins, but at least one PC is downed (unconscious, gravely wounded, or barely standing); the cost is severe.
- "routed" — the party does NOT win the fight, but the result is RECOVERABLE: a forced retreat, capture, a desperate flight, or being downed and left for dead. The party survives to continue. PC death is rare and only plausible here. Never default to "everyone dies" / a total party kill — that is forbidden as a baseline outcome.

Outcomes must be:
- One to two sentences each
- General narrative constraints, not specific dialogue or pre-written prose
- Written so the DM has creative freedom in how they narrate the result
- Matched to the world tone above
