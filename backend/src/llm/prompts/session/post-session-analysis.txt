The `summary` and `emergingThreads` are prose you author — write them in the register below. (It does NOT touch the verbatim `voice` and `atmosphere` fields, which are quoted exactly from the transcript, never restyled.)

{{include:shared/prose-register}}

`summary`: a tight recap of what the party did and what visibly happened, player-witnessed only. Shape it to the session's movement: 1-4 SHORT paragraphs (2-4 sentences each), separated by a blank line, broken where the session itself breaks — a discovery, a confrontation, a journey, a retreat. A session that was one continuous scene may be a single paragraph; a session with several distinct movements must NOT be compressed into one dense block. The paragraph breaks are structural only — same words, same register as a single paragraph would carry.

`emergingThreads`: the open questions and tensions the players themselves are now chasing or have visibly stumbled into. Player-witnessed only — no prep-only threads.

`handoff` primes the next session's prep. It MAY be secret-aware (it feeds the prep pipeline, not the player-facing surface):
- `handoff.party`: one entry per PC — that character's EXACT end-of-session `location` and `condition` (wounds, status, resources) as of the final transcript moment.
- `handoff.timeOfDay`: the in-world time at session end.
- `handoff.unresolvedThreads`: open threads carried into the next session, MOST URGENT FIRST.
- `handoff.npcs`: MAJOR NPCs only (~6 max). For each:
  - `state`: the NPC's CURRENT disposition toward the party as of session end (how they now stand relative to the PCs). This is a paraphrasable prep-prompt input.
  - `voice`: an array of about 2 VERBATIM excerpts of the NPC's own lines from the transcript. Quote EXACTLY — never paraphrase, never invent, never stitch fragments together. Choose lines that show how this NPC TREATS, FEELS ABOUT, or ACTS AROUND the PCs — the relational texture, not generic flavor. Prefer LATE-session lines: they capture the EVOLVED relationship. NEVER pick a generic first-impression or stereotype line (e.g. a gruff "coin first, stories after") — that flattens the character and resets all the development this session earned. NEVER quote a `[[DM CORRECTION:]]` retcon line or a farewell wind-down line.
- `handoff.keyLocations`: the session's notable locations (~6 max). For each, a VERBATIM sensory `atmosphere` snippet drawn from the transcript prose.
