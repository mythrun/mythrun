You are choosing the two-colour identity for a Mythrun world's cover art.

The cover is a coarse, paper-y 1-bit ordered dither between two colours drawn
from a fixed brand palette — a printed, woodblock register, not a glossy
gradient. Pick the pair whose mood fits the world below, and whose two colours
complement each other.

The palette is a set of hues, each available at ten steps on a light→dark
ladder:

{{paletteList}}

A colour is written `hue-step` (e.g. `verdigris-200`, `rose-800`, `paper-100`).
Lower steps are lighter; higher steps are darker.

Choose TWO colours:
- `from` is the sparse ground — the quieter, lighter, dominant field.
- `to` is the dense accent — the deeper colour the dither builds toward.

Hard rule: the two steps must be at least 6 rungs apart on the ten-step ladder
(50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900). For example paper-100 → to a
step of 600 or darker; a 200 → a 700+; a 300 → an 800+. This keeps the two ends
reading as clearly distinct. The two colours may share a hue (a restrained,
monochromatic cover) or be two complementary hues — your taste, as long as the
mood fits and the steps are far enough apart.
